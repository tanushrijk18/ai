Four modules

Everything the transport office and students need

MODULE 01

User & Admin Management

Secure-looking sign in and registration with separate student and admin dashboards.

MODULE 02

Route & Schedule Management

Search routes by number, bus or stop. Admins add, edit and remove routes and timings.

MODULE 03

Transport Pass Management

Apply online, track Pending / Approved / Rejected status, and get remarks from the admin.

MODULE 04

Feedback & Complaints

Rate a service, raise a complaint and follow its progress through to resolution.

How it works

From sign-up to boarding in three steps

1

Sign in to your role

Students and administrators each land on their own dashboard.

2

Find a route & apply

Pick your route and pickup stop, choose a pass type and submit.

3

Track and give feedback

See the admin's decision instantly and tell us how the service is doing.
